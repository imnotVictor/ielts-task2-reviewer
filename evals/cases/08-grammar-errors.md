## Task 2 question

Some people prefer living in a large city, while others prefer living in the countryside. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

## Student essay

There is many differences between life in a big city and life in countryside. Some people choosing cities because it provide more chances, while other people likes quiet places. In my opinion, city life is more suitable for young adults, but countryside can be better after people become older.

People who prefer cities often wants convenient services and more jobs. A large city have hospitals, universities, shops and many kinds of company. If a person lose one job, they may finds another work more quickly. Public transport are also available, so residents does not always need a car. However, these advantages comes with high costs. Rent and food is expensive, and workers may spending a long time travelling between home and office.

On the other hand, countryside areas is usually quieter and less polluted. Families can live in a larger house, and children has more space for playing outside. Neighbours may knows each other and gives help when someone have a problem. Nevertheless, employment choices can be limited. A young person who want a specialised career may needing to move away. Medical care are sometimes far, which create difficulties for older residents.

For myself, I would choose a city at the beginning of my career because it offer education, jobs and different experiences. Later in life, I might move to a small town near a city. This choice would giving me a quieter environment while keeping important services within reach.

In conclusion, cities provide opportunity and convenience, whereas countryside offers space and peace. The best choice depend on a person’s age, work and priorities.
