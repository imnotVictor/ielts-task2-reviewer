# Case 04: Weak Paragraph Progression

## Task 2 question

Working from home is becoming increasingly common. Do the advantages of this development outweigh the disadvantages?

## Student essay

Remote work has expanded because digital tools allow employees to communicate from almost anywhere. Although this development offers some benefits, I believe its disadvantages are slightly greater, especially for new employees and for people who live in unsuitable homes.

Working from home saves commuting time. Employees can begin their duties without spending an hour on a train, and companies can reduce office costs. However, young workers need to learn professional behaviour from experienced colleagues. Video calls are useful for meetings. Some people also eat more healthily at home because they can cook lunch. Offices are expensive in city centres, so companies can hire workers from other regions. New staff may be afraid to ask small questions online, which can slow their progress.

Another issue is the home environment. A parent may not have a quiet room, and neighbours or children can interrupt important calls. This can reduce concentration. On the other hand, workers can choose comfortable clothes and control the room temperature. Businesses may also reduce pollution if fewer employees travel. Nevertheless, some managers do not trust remote staff and arrange too many online meetings. Long periods alone can make workers feel isolated, while flexible hours may help them attend medical appointments.

Overall, remote work has clear practical advantages, but these do not benefit everyone equally. The loss of informal learning and the difficulty of finding a suitable workspace can have lasting effects. For this reason, I believe the disadvantages slightly outweigh the advantages, although a hybrid arrangement may reduce many of the problems.

## Evaluation target

Primary weakness: The overall position is clear, but the body paragraphs jump between unrelated advantages and disadvantages instead of developing one controlled purpose.

Available evidence anchors:

- “Video calls are useful for meetings. Some people also eat more healthily at home”
- “Offices are expensive in city centres”
- “workers can choose comfortable clothes and control the room temperature”

Unsupported diagnosis to avoid: The conclusion contradicts the introduction.
