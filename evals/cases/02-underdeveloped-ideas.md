# Case 02: Relevant but Underdeveloped Ideas

## Task 2 question

University education should be free for everyone, regardless of their financial background. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

## Student essay

Many people believe that students should not pay for university. I completely agree because education is important for society and tuition fees are unfair to poor families.

First, free university education can make a country better. Educated people are useful, and every country needs useful people. If more students attend university, there will be more graduates. These graduates can have jobs and help the economy. For example, doctors and engineers are important in every place. Therefore, the government should pay the tuition fees so that there can be more doctors and engineers.

Second, tuition fees create inequality. Rich students can attend good universities, but poor students may not have enough money. This situation is unfair because poor students can also be intelligent. If education is free, all students will have the same chance. They can study hard and get a degree. This will make society equal, which is a good thing for everyone. In addition, students will be happier because they will not worry about money.

Some people say that free university will cost the government too much. However, governments spend money on many things, and education is more important. They can simply move money from less important areas. Also, graduates will pay tax in the future, so the money will return.

In conclusion, I strongly believe that university should be free. It improves the country, helps poor students and makes people happier. Governments must provide this opportunity to every person who wants a degree.

## Evaluation target

Primary weakness: The position is clear and relevant, but several ideas are asserted in general terms without explaining the mechanism, limits, or practical trade-offs.

Available evidence anchors:

- “Educated people are useful, and every country needs useful people.”
- “This will make society equal, which is a good thing for everyone.”
- “They can simply move money from less important areas.”

Unsupported diagnosis to avoid: The essay fails to answer whether the writer agrees or disagrees.
