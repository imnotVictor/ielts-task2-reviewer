# Case 10: Near the Target Level

## Task 2 question

Some museums charge an entrance fee, while others are free. Do you think the advantages of charging visitors outweigh the disadvantages?

## Student essay

Museums must preserve collections, employ specialist staff and provide a safe environment for visitors. Although entrance fees can reduce public access, I believe moderate charges generally bring greater benefits when museums also offer discounts or free periods.

The main advantage is a more reliable source of income. Public funding can change when governments face other priorities, whereas ticket revenue is linked directly to museum use. This money can support conservation work, improve exhibitions and train guides. Visitors may then receive a better experience rather than seeing poorly maintained displays. Charging a fee can also help museums control extremely busy periods because people are more likely to plan their visits in advance.

The clearest disadvantage is that a fee may exclude low-income families. Museums hold objects and knowledge that form part of a shared cultural heritage, so access should not depend entirely on a person’s ability to pay. If parents avoid museums because several tickets are expensive, children lose an educational opportunity. Free entry can also encourage spontaneous visits and allow residents to return to the same exhibition more than once.

However, charging and accessibility are not necessarily opposites. Museums can offer free admission to children, students and local residents, as well as one free evening each week. A modest ticket price for other visitors could then support the institution without creating a complete financial barrier. Tourist destinations may also charge international visitors while keeping community programmes free.

In conclusion, entrance fees provide useful and relatively stable income, but they should be designed carefully. With targeted discounts and regular free access, the financial advantages of charging visitors outweigh the disadvantages.
