# Case 01: Partial and Off-Topic Response

## Task 2 question

Some people think governments should spend more money on public transport, while others believe building and improving roads is more important. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

## Student essay

Traffic has become a serious concern in many large cities, and it affects the daily lives of workers and students. In my opinion, the most effective way to solve this issue is to encourage companies to let their staff work from home. This approach would reduce the number of people travelling during busy hours and would also improve family life.

The first reason is that office workers create a large part of the morning traffic. Thousands of employees travel to the same business areas at nearly the same time, so streets and trains become crowded. If companies allowed three or four remote-working days each week, many journeys would disappear. Workers could save time and use it for exercise or childcare. Companies might also rent smaller offices and reduce their electricity bills.

Another useful solution is changing school and office starting times. At present, parents, students and workers often leave home within the same thirty-minute period. Schools could begin earlier, while offices could offer flexible arrival times. This would spread travel across the morning instead of forcing everyone onto the transport system at once. In addition, online meetings can replace unnecessary business trips between different parts of a city.

In conclusion, urban traffic cannot be solved simply by asking people to be more patient. Remote work, flexible schedules and online meetings can reduce the need to travel and make cities more comfortable. Governments should persuade employers to adopt these policies as soon as possible.
